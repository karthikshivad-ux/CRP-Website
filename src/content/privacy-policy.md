# CRP GROWTH — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 18 October 2026

**Last Updated:** 18 October 2026

## 1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how CRP GROWTH collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit the website, register for membership, make a UPI payment, or contact us. CRP GROWTH operates in India and handles personal information subject to applicable law.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect:

- **Name and place**, to identify your membership and administer the service.
- **Phone and WhatsApp numbers**, to contact you about registration, payment verification, support, and delivery of membership access instructions. You may provide the same number for both fields.
- **Business name**, if you choose to provide it.
- **Selected membership plan and amount**, to create and administer your membership and payment record.

We also maintain information generated while administering a membership, which may include a member code, membership status, start and expiry dates, and creation or update timestamps.

CRP GROWTH administrators may create or update a membership record when assisting with registration, membership changes, or payment verification. The admin area also processes administrator account and session information needed to restrict access to authorized staff.

For payment reconciliation, we maintain the payment reference generated at registration and the payment amount, currency, selected plan, payment method (UPI), and payment status. When staff verify a payment, the record may also include the verified transaction reference, payment date, and a receipt link if one is supplied. CRP GROWTH does not request or store your UPI PIN, bank password, or UPI-app login credentials.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- create and administer membership and payment records;
- match and verify UPI payments, activate membership, and set membership dates;
- send membership access instructions and service-related communications to the WhatsApp contact you provide;
- respond to questions, payment issues, and complaints;
- protect the service, investigate suspected fraud or misuse, and meet legal obligations.

The current site does not provide a member login, member dashboard, or member OTP authentication. Admin authentication is restricted to authorized CRP GROWTH administrators.

## 4. UPI Payments and Other Payment Participants

The website creates a pending payment record and provides links that open an external UPI app. The payment is completed in the app selected by you. UPI apps, banks, and payment service providers involved in the transfer process payment information under their own terms and privacy practices. Please review the notices provided by those services.

CRP GROWTH receives and records the details needed to reconcile and verify the payment, such as the amount, UPI payment reference, status, and, when verified by staff, the transaction reference and payment date. We do not currently use Razorpay as the website's payment processor.

## 5. Service Providers and Storage

CRP GROWTH uses **Supabase** for database and backend services and for authentication used by the admin area. Relevant membership, payment, and administrator records may be stored or processed by Supabase under the applicable service configuration. See [Supabase's privacy notice](https://supabase.com/privacy).

The website and its server-side API routes are deployed using **Vercel**. Vercel may process information needed to deliver the site and API requests and to maintain operational and security logs, according to the deployment configuration and Vercel's policies. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

We limit access to membership and payment records to authorized personnel who need them to operate the service, verify payments, or provide support.

## 6. When We Share Information

We may share information as needed with:

- Supabase and Vercel to host and operate the website, database, APIs, and admin authentication;
- the UPI app, banks, and payment service providers involved in a payment, as applicable to the transaction;
- authorized CRP GROWTH personnel who administer memberships, payments, and support;
- government authorities, courts, regulators, or other parties where required or permitted by law.

We do not sell members' personal information as a commercial data product.

## 7. Retention

We retain information while it is reasonably needed to provide and administer membership, reconcile payments, respond to disputes or complaints, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the record and applicable requirements. When information is no longer needed, we will take appropriate steps to delete or dispose of it, subject to any legal retention obligation or unresolved dispute.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your membership or payment information has been exposed, contact us using the details below. Do not send us your UPI PIN, bank password, or payment-app credentials.

## 9. Cookies and Technical Information

The current site uses authentication session cookies for the admin area. These cookies are HttpOnly and use the SameSite=Lax setting. They support authorized administrator sessions; they are not member login cookies. We do not describe them as analytics or advertising cookies.

Vercel and other infrastructure providers may process technical request information in operational or security logs. The details and retention of such logs depend on the service configuration and provider settings.

## 10. Processing Locations

Supabase, Vercel, UPI apps, banks, or other service providers may process information in locations determined by their service configurations. We will handle any applicable transfer or access in accordance with applicable law and the relevant provider arrangements.

## 11. Privacy Requests and Complaints

To ask a question or submit a privacy request or complaint, contact CRP GROWTH using the email or phone below. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant membership. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity before acting. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and any legal retention requirements.

Where applicable law provides rights concerning personal information, including correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, you may submit the request using these contact details. Withdrawal may affect our ability to provide a membership service that depends on the information. It does not affect processing already carried out or records we must retain under law.

## 12. Children's Information

Please contact us if you believe a child's personal information was submitted to CRP GROWTH. We will review the matter and take steps required by applicable law. Any age or consent requirements that apply to membership registration must be followed.

## 13. Communications

We may use the phone or WhatsApp contact you provide for membership access instructions, payment or membership updates, and support. Any promotional communications will be handled subject to applicable law and relevant preferences or consent.

## 14. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may open an external UPI app or link to third-party services. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy notices. CRP GROWTH does not control their independent privacy practices.

## 15. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the service, data handling, providers, or applicable law. The latest version will show its effective and updated dates. Where required, we will provide notice or obtain consent before a change takes effect.
